Open the poll on When2meet

Fill When2meet from your calendar, in one click.

Connect Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar link once. On every When2meet poll, click Fill from my calendar, check the preview, and save.

Get the extension Source code

Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Free and open source (MIT). Unofficial: not affiliated with When2meet.

How it works

  1. 1. Connect calendars.
    Google, Microsoft 365 / Outlook, or a calendar link from Apple iCloud, Proton, Fastmail and others. Use as many as you like.
  2. 2. Open any poll.
    A Fill from my calendar button appears on When2meet pages. It signs in with your name.
  3. 3. Check and save.
    Changes are highlighted on your grid. Save, and the result is checked against When2meet. Undo is one click.

Built to stay out of the way

  • Updates on revisit. If your calendar changes, reopening the poll offers a one-click update. Edits you made by hand are kept.
  • Your rules. Buffers around meetings, working hours, how to treat tentative and all-day events.
  • Best times. See the windows that work for the most people and add one to Google, Outlook or any calendar.
  • Private by design. Your calendar is read in your browser and only free/busy times are used. Nothing is stored on our servers.

Privacy

The extension reads only free/busy information (Google) or basic event times (Microsoft), computes your availability in your browser, and sends When2meet only the slots you choose to save, under the name you give. See the privacy policy.
